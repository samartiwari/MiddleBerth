Pay for your berth

This opens Razorpay's real checkout in test mode. No money moves.

Use one of Razorpay's domestic test cards, any future expiry, any CVV, and click Success on the bank screen:

  • Visa debit — 4100 2800 0000 1007
  • Mastercard credit — 5555 5100 0008 1006

Not 4111 1111 1111 1111. That is a generic dummy number and Razorpay reads it as an international card, which most Indian test accounts have switched off — it fails with “International cards are not supported”.

Close this tab when you are done. The script in the terminal is watching for the payment and will carry on by itself.
